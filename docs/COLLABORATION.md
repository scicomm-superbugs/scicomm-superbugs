# Collaborative Science Communication Guidelines

Welcome to the Science Communication and Biotechnology initiative.

## Collaborative Principles
- **Clarity & Evidence**: Translate complex molecular and computational concepts into accessible, verifiable insights.
- **Peer Review & Co-authorship**: We encourage active collaboration across interdisciplinary student projects, academic departments, and laboratory cohorts.
- **Open Access**: Educational software suites and public data analytics tools are openly shared to foster student learning.
